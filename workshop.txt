version=1
title=Wheelbarrow
description=A construction wheelbarrow for hauling the loads Project Zomboid normally punishes you for carrying: generators, logs, propane tanks.
description=
description=[b]It is deliberately useless for ordinary loot.[/b] Weight reduction only applies to genuinely heavy items - filling it with nails and canned food gains you nothing. It exists for a handful of very heavy objects, and it charges you for that: it takes [b]both hands[/b], so no weapon while you push it, and it slows you down.
description=
description=Picking it up and setting it down is a timed action. Interrupt it and the wheelbarrow tips over, dumping everything on the ground.
description=
description=[b]It is never in your inventory.[/b] Like a vehicle, it is always either on the ground or in your hands - [b]E[/b] picks it up and sets it down. It also stops you climbing fences, going through windows and getting into cars, and it is set down automatically while you do anything else, then picked back up.
description=
description=[h1]Getting one[/h1]
description=Find one lying around construction sites, warehouses, garages and tool stores, or at roadworks marked with cones. One roll per place, once per save - they are meant to be a find, not a given.
description=
description=Or weld your own: sheet metal, pipe, a car tyre and screws, at [b]MetalWelding level 4[/b]. Not an early-game build.
description=
description=[b]Everything is configurable[/b] in the Sandbox options - 12 of them: total capacity, a separate much lower ceiling for light loads, what counts as heavy, how much weight is relieved, action length, spawn rate, crafting, and whether the tip over happens at all. In multiplayer the server decides for everyone.
description=
description=[b]Languages:[/b] 23, including English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Czech, Hungarian, Norwegian, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
description=
description=[b]No other mods required.[/b]
description=
description=[h1]Removing the mod[/h1]
description=Removing it from a save that already has wheelbarrows in the world can cause errors on load. That is how Project Zomboid handles items whose definition disappeared, not something specific to this mod. Empty and destroy your wheelbarrows before uninstalling.
